## Overview

This is the first piece in Johann Sebastian Bach's "The Well-Tempered Clavier, Book I".
It is composed entirely of arpeggios (broken chords) throughout, possessing a simple yet profound harmonic progression.

### Structure

The piece is based on the following harmonic progression:

1. Tonic (I)
2. Supertonic (ii)
3. Dominant (V)
4. Tonic (I)

Charles Gounod composed his "Ave Maria" using this piece as accompaniment.

## Performance Tips

- **Legato**: It is required to play smoothly while maintaining evenness of tone.
- **Pedal**: When playing on a modern piano, careful pedaling is necessary to avoid muddy harmonies.

## Analysis

This is the first piece in Volume 1 of J.S. Bach's "The Well-Tempered Clavier".
Composed entirely of arpeggios, it features a simple yet profoundly deep harmonic progression.

### Structure

The piece is built on the following chord progression:

```abc
X:1
T:Prelude in C Major (Theme)
C:J.S. Bach
M:4/4
L:1/16
K:C
CEGce cGE C E G c e c G E |
```

Charles Gounod notoriously used this piece as an accompaniment for his "Ave Maria".

## Performance Tips

- **Legato Playing**: Aim for evenness and a smooth connection between notes.
- **Pedaling**: On a modern piano, careful pedaling is essential to avoid blurring the harmonies.
